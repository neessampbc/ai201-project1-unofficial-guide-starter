Professor Aaron Goldstein — Philosophy (PHILO 101, Introduction to Philosophy) — CUNY Hunter College
NOTE: Synthetic student reviews created for an educational RAG project. These are not real reviews of a real person.

Rating: 4/5 | Difficulty: 3/5 | Would take again: Yes
Goldstein runs the class like a Socratic discussion. He cold-calls, so do the reading. The grade is two argumentative papers and a final — no exams to memorize. He cares about the logic of your argument, not your conclusion.

Rating: 4.5/5 | Difficulty: 3/5 | Would take again: Yes
If you like to argue, this class is a blast. We debated free will, ethics, and the existence of knowledge. Goldstein rewards original thinking. The papers are short (5 pages) but he expects tight reasoning.

Rating: 2.5/5 | Difficulty: 3/5 | Would take again: Maybe
The readings (Descartes, Hume, Kant) are dense and slow going. Goldstein is patient unpacking them in lecture, but if you skip the reading you will be lost during the cold calls. Participation is 20% of the grade.

Rating: 5/5 | Difficulty: 2/5 | Would take again: Yes
Made me actually enjoy philosophy. No busywork, just two papers and class discussion. Goldstein gives detailed written feedback on your first paper so you know exactly how to improve for the second.

Rating: 3.5/5 | Difficulty: 3/5 | Would take again: Yes
Discussion-based and fair. The challenge is the writing, not the workload volume. Each paper has to defend a thesis against an objection. Use his office hours to test your argument before you write it up.
