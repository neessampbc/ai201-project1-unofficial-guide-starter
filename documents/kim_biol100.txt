Rating: 3/5 | Difficulty: 4/5 | Would take again: Maybe
BIOL 100 with Kim is a memorization marathon. Expect to learn every organ system in detail. Flashcards are your best friend. The lecture exams are mostly diagrams you have to label and short answer.

Rating: 2.5/5 | Difficulty: 4/5 | Would take again: No
The lab is a separate grade and it is a lot of work — weekly lab reports due 48 hours after each session. Kim's lectures are clear but the sheer volume of terms is overwhelming if you fall behind even one week.

Rating: 4/5 | Difficulty: 3/5 | Would take again: Yes
Kim is a kind professor who clearly wants you to succeed. He posts recorded lectures so you can rewatch the hard parts. Heavy on memorization but there are no trick questions — study the labeled diagrams and you're set.

Rating: 4.5/5 | Difficulty: 3/5 | Would take again: Yes
Great for pre-nursing students. The material is directly relevant and Kim ties everything back to real health examples. Just stay on top of the lab reports — they sneak up on you and are 30% of the grade.

Rating: 3.5/5 | Difficulty: 4/5 | Would take again: Maybe
Lots of memorization, lab-heavy, but doable with consistent effort. The practical exam (identifying structures on models) is harder than the written exams. Go to the open lab hours to practice with the actual models.
