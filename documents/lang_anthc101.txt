Professor Robert Lang — Anthropology (ANTHC 101, Introduction to Cultural Anthropology) — CUNY Hunter College
NOTE: Synthetic student reviews created for an educational RAG project. These are not real reviews of a real person.

Rating: 3.5/5 | Difficulty: 3/5 | Would take again: Yes
Lang's class is reading-heavy ethnographies but the lectures are fascinating travel-story style. Two exams that mix multiple choice with one essay, plus a fieldwork observation assignment where you observe a public space and write it up.

Rating: 4/5 | Difficulty: 3/5 | Would take again: Yes
The fieldwork project is the highlight — you do a mini-ethnography of somewhere in NYC. Lang gives great feedback. Exams are fair if you go to lecture; he tells you which concepts will show up.

Rating: 2.5/5 | Difficulty: 3/5 | Would take again: Maybe
The ethnography readings are long and sometimes dry. Lang is engaging in person but the workload is mostly reading. The essay portion of the exams expects you to cite specific examples from those readings, so skimming won't cut it.

Rating: 4.5/5 | Difficulty: 2/5 | Would take again: Yes
One of my favorite classes. Lang brings in artifacts and tells stories from his own fieldwork. Grading is transparent: a rubric for everything. The fieldwork paper is more fun than work.

Rating: 3/5 | Difficulty: 3/5 | Would take again: Yes
Solid professor. Reading-heavy but no tricks. Two midterms, no cumulative final, plus the observation paper. If you do the readings and attend lecture, a B+ or better is very achievable.
