Rating: 4/5 | Difficulty: 2/5 | Would take again: Yes
Okafor's exams are all multiple choice, pulled from the textbook and lecture. There are three exams and they are not cumulative. If you read the chapter summaries and do the practice quizzes on Blackboard you'll do great.

Rating: 4.5/5 | Difficulty: 2/5 | Would take again: Yes
There are short attendance quizzes at the start of most lectures — usually one or two questions on the previous class. They are easy points but you have to actually show up. Miss them and your grade drops fast even if you ace the exams.

Rating: 3/5 | Difficulty: 2/5 | Would take again: Maybe
Large lecture hall, so it can feel impersonal. But the grading is straightforward: multiple-choice exams plus attendance quizzes plus one short paper. No surprises. Good class for a non-major checking the box.

Rating: 5/5 | Difficulty: 1/5 | Would take again: Yes
Easiest science gen-ed at Hunter. Okafor literally tells you what's on the exam in the review session. Multiple choice, no essays on the tests. The only way to do badly is to skip the attendance quizzes.
