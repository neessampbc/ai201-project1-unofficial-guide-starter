Rivera's exams come almost entirely from his lecture slides, not the Savitch textbook. If you go to every lecture and rewrite the slide code by hand, you will be fine. He curves the midterm but not the final, so don't slack off at the end.

Rating: 5/5 | Difficulty: 3/5 | Would take again: Yes
Best intro professor in the CS department. Office hours are packed but worth it. He explains pointers and recursion better than any YouTube video. Midterm was curved by almost a full letter grade.

Rating: 3.5/5 | Difficulty: 3/5 | Would take again: Yes

Rating: 4/5 | Difficulty: 2/5 | Would take again: Yes
